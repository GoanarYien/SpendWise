How to use SpendWise effectively
  1. Set your target: Click Set Monthly Budget in the sidebar to enter your monthly spending limit.
  2. Log every transaction: Enter the item name, amount, category, and date in the Add Expense form.
  3. Watch your status banner: SpendWise automatically warns you when you pass 80% of your budget or exceed your limit.
  4. Filter or remove items: Use the category dropdown above the table to inspect specific spending areas or delete mistakes.
